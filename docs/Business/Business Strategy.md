# Business strategy

## Thesis
Roblox distribution now rewards **long-term retention** (the D8-28 play days signal, from June 2026) and **session length** (Creator Rewards pay 5 R$ per qualified user with a 10+ minute session). A small AI-assisted studio wins by shipping **one polished, retention-focused game** in a proven genre, measuring it, then compounding. It does not win by spraying many shallow games.

## Why one game first (and not many)
- Recommended For You ranking uses only organic players' behaviour. Each game has to earn its own signals, and weak games don't build up.
- Clones of trending formats saturate within weeks. Quality and differentiation are what survive.
- The tooling built here (harness, data, receipts, UI kit; see [[Reusable Systems]]) makes game #2 much cheaper **once** game #1 has taught us what retains.

## Phases
1. **Validate (weeks 0-4).** Studio test, private test, soft launch. Goal: D1 ≥ 20%, D7 ≥ 7%, median session ≥ 12 minutes.
2. **Improve (weeks 4-12).** Weekly fixes, a Halloween event, the first new zone. Goal: stable organic Recommended For You impressions.
3. **Monetize and scale (month 3+).** Price tests, bundles, maybe small ad campaigns (with approval). Goal: DevEx-eligible monthly earnings (30K+ R$).
4. **Expand (month 4+, only if the data supports it).** Game #2 reuses the systems, informed by what worked (see [[Future Concepts]]).

## Competitive position
| We have | They (typical clones) have |
|---|---|
| Real co-op payoffs (zone clears, squad bonus, Golden Storm) | Solo grind |
| Long zone path, rebirth, perks, 24 critters with golden versions | 1-2 hours of content |
| No paid gacha, no pop-ups, transparent odds | Aggressive prompts |
| Automated tests and server-authoritative economy | Exploitable remotes |

## Risks
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| The cleaning trend fades | Core systems are theme-agnostic; seasonal and new zones keep freshness |
| Low discovery at launch | Thumbnail A/B tests, short videos, small search-ad tests after gates |
| Unverified in-engine behaviour | Studio checklist first; the automated suite catches regressions |
| Platform policy changes | No regulated mechanics; check the DevForum announcements monthly |
